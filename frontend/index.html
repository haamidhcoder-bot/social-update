Tamil Nadu's food notice board

Extra food belongs on a plate, not in the bin.

Social Update is a simple place for people who grow, cook and give food to find each other. Farmers list what they've harvested. Hotels and colleges post what they need, and tell nearby orphanages and NGOs when there's a good meal going spare.

example Surplus food

Veg biryani, about 40 plates

Left over from a college function. Cooked at 12:30 pm, safe to serve until 4:30 pm. Pick-up from the canteen.

example Needed

25 kg onions, any week

Small hotel near the bus stand. Happy to collect from a farmer within 20 km.

How it works

Post what you have. See what's nearby. Call each other.

1

Farmers list their harvest

Say what you've grown, how much, and what price you'd like. Hotels, colleges and factories in your district can see it straight away.

2

Buyers say what they need

Kitchens and processing units post the ingredients or raw material they're looking for, so farmers know who to call.

3

Spare food finds a home

When there's safe food left over, share it with a countdown. Orphanages and NGOs nearby can pick it up before it spoils.
